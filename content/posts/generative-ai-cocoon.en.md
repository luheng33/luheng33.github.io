# The problem

Most research on AI today studies how people use AI.

But look closely and you will find that the AI economists see, and the AI people mention, is mostly **generative AI**. Generative AI is only one part of artificial intelligence, and research has not yet told the two apart.

If the use of generative AI is studied as if it were AI itself, the conclusions are easy to doubt.

# Why call it an information cocoon

What we see is almost entirely generative AI, so we come to think that AI means generative AI. Researchers' field of view is enclosed by the one kind of AI that is easiest for them to see.
